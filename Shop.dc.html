HOW A JAR GETS MADE

Four steps, and the fourth is the one most brands skip.

Read the method
  • 01 Actives kept apart PDRN and copper peptides lose potency when they sit pre-mixed in one emulsion. Holding each in its own bead keeps them apart until you apply.
  • 02 Cold-fill at 24°C GHK-Cu denatures above roughly 40°C. Our filling line never exceeds 24°C, which rules out the hot-emulsion process most creams use.
  • 03 Lot-level assay Every batch is assayed for actives concentration and microbial load before release. The report is what your lot code opens.
  • 04 Batch report published The certificate is posted the day the batch ships. Nothing is held back, including batches that came in at the low end of our range.

THE ROUTINE

Three steps, twice a day.

Simple, consistent care for stronger, healthier-looking skin.

STEP 01

Serum first

Three peptides in a water-light layer. Press it into damp skin and give it a moment to settle before anything else goes on.

STEP 02

Then the cream

A small amount over the serum. The pink and blue beads break as you smooth it in, so the two actives meet your skin, not each other in the jar.

STEP 03

Morning and evening

The same two steps, twice a day. Follow with sunscreen in the morning. Every jar carries a lot code that opens its own batch report.

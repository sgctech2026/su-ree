We ship nationwide across the Philippines from our Makati warehouse. Orders placed before 2pm on a business day are picked and handed to the courier the same day.

Delivery times

Timeframes below start the business day after dispatch and exclude weekends and public holidays.

Peak periods such as 11.11, 12.12 and the weeks before Christmas add two to three days across all zones. We post a notice on the homepage when that is in effect.

Dispatch and cut-off

Our cut-off is 2:00 PM Manila time, Monday to Saturday. Orders after that are dispatched the next business day.

  • Orders are packed in the order they are paid for.
  • Cash-on-delivery orders are confirmed by SMS before dispatch.
  • We do not dispatch on Sundays or public holidays.

Tracking your parcel

The moment your parcel is scanned by the courier, we email and SMS a tracking link. The same link lives in your account under My Orders.

If tracking has not moved for three business days, that usually means a courier scan was missed rather than a lost parcel. Message us and we will chase it.

Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is available nationwide with no extra handling fee. Please have the exact amount ready, as couriers do not reliably carry change.

If a COD parcel is refused on arrival, the order is cancelled and returned to us. Repeated refusals mean we may ask for prepayment on future orders.

Failed deliveries

Couriers attempt delivery up to three times. After the third attempt the parcel comes back to our warehouse and we will contact you to arrange a re-ship.

Re-shipping after a failed delivery is charged at the standard rate for your zone, unless the failure was our error.

International orders

We do not ship outside the Philippines yet. Singapore and Malaysia are planned for 2026.

If you are abroad and want to be told when we open your market, join the list from any product page.

Need a person instead of a policy?

Write to hello@su-ree.com with your order number and we will sort it out.

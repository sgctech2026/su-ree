OUR STORY

Because your skin deservesmore than promises.

Taking care of your skin is an act of choosing yourself. And when it comes to something so personal, you deserve to know exactly what you’re choosing.

In 2023, our founder purchased a peptide cream that cost nearly as much as a week’s worth of groceries. She had one simple question: What exactly is inside this jar?

After three unanswered emails, all she received was a familiar response: proprietary blends.

That experience sparked a bigger question: Why should knowing what’s inside your skincare be so difficult?

That question became the beginning of su-ree.

We believe skincare should be built on honesty, not empty promises. That’s why every su-ree jar comes with a unique lot code that gives you access to its assay results, fill date, and manufacturing facility.

Because self-care should come with confidence. Because your trust should never be taken for granted. And because you deserve to know what you put on your skin.

su-ree. Skincare you can know, trust, and feel good about.
